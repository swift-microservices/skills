PASS if the design states which module or service owns which data, with no table read or joined by two of them, and identifiers generated by the owning database; in a monolith the modules keep their own tables in one database, and across services each has its own database (placing them on one instance, or a schema per service, is a placement choice, not a violation).

FAIL if two modules or services share tables, one reads another's database or schema, or a distributed transaction is proposed.
