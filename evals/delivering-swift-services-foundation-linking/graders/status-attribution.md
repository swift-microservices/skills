PASS if the note attributes the application's full-Foundation linkage to its required dependencies Vapor 4.122.2 and PostgresNIO 1.33.1, keeps both, and says the executable's linkage is rechecked whenever the resolved dependencies change.
FAIL if it puts an Essentials-only linking gate on the application (which is guaranteed to fail), invents a trait that removes Foundation from Vapor or PostgresNIO, replaces the database or the framework, or claims a static SDK build proves the absence of Foundation.
